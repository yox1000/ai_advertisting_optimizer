FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the furniture include?

The included Loft furniture is made of five sets, each with one white leather couch and one glass coffee table. The Alcove furniture includes three white leather couches and one coffee table. The Terrace includes one navy velour sofa, three grey ottomans, a round wooden coffee table, and a mahogany bar.

What standard tables are included?

The venue includes two 6 ft and two 8 ft standard buffet tables in the Loft, and four 6 ft standard buffet tables on the Terrace. All other tables and chairs must be rented.

What are the measurements of the furniture?

The Loft bar base and top are approximately 86 in x 33.5 in x 45.5 in. The Terrace mahogany bar top is about 4 ft 6 in by 2 ft, with a 3 ft 4 in height.

What is the sound system compatibility?

DJs are not allowed to hook into the venue system and must provide their own equipment. The venue provides a wireless microphone for the Loft and a small mixer for basic use.

Is there outside lighting?

Yes, the Terrace includes outside lighting for evening events.

Where is the freight elevator located?

The freight elevator is located on 1/2 East 29th Street between Madison and 5th Avenue, closer to 5th Avenue, at the loading dock between the church and bank on the right hand side of the street.

Can we make an outside bar?

Yes, using the venue's standard buffet tables. Leg extensions would need to be added.

What are the ceiling heights?

The ceiling is 12 ft high and approximately 11 ft high at the beams.

What is the scheduling for rental pick-up and drop-off?

All rentals and deliveries must be dropped off between 9am-12noon and 2pm-5pm, and must be picked up after the event before the contracted breakdown time.
